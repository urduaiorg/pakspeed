About PakSpeed

Our Mission

PakSpeed was developed by the creative engineers at WANG (Welfare Association for New Generation) and Urdu AI with a singular vision: to bring a beautiful, lightning-fast, and fully localized internet speed testing tool to Pakistan. We realized that existing global tools lacked proper Urdu translation and failed to give Pakistani users a centralized dashboard comparing local ISPs directly against one another.

Our City Rankings and ISP Comparison pages aim to create total transparency. By combining your real-world speed test results with geographically clustered data, we empower everyday consumers to answer questions like: "Is Zong really faster than Jazz in Lahore?" or "Should I switch to StormFiber in Karachi?"

Methodology

We believe in full transparency about how we measure internet speed. Here is our step-by-step process:

1

Cloudflare Edge CDN

Tests run against Cloudflare's global edge network with PoPs in Pakistani cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad) for accurate local measurements.

2

Web Worker Threading

Speed tests run in a dedicated browser Web Worker thread, preventing UI interference and ensuring accurate throughput measurement.

3

Multi-Stream Testing

Multiple parallel TCP connections saturate your link, measuring true available bandwidth just like real-world usage patterns.

4

Crowdsourced Data

Results are aggregated from real users across Pakistan. No synthetic tests. Every data point comes from an actual speed test.

What We Measure

  1. Ping (Latency): Round-trip time for a signal to travel to the nearest Cloudflare edge server and back. Measured in milliseconds (ms). Lower is better. Under 30ms is excellent for gaming and video calls.
  2. Jitter: The variance in ping measurements over time. Low jitter means a stable, consistent connection. High jitter causes lag spikes and video buffering.
  3. Download Speed: Measured in Megabits per second (Mbps). How quickly you receive data from the internet. Determines streaming quality, file download times, and page load speeds.
  4. Upload Speed: Also in Mbps. How quickly you send data to the internet. Critical for video calling, live streaming, uploading files, and cloud backups.

Data Transparency

Every test result includes ISP identification (via IP geolocation), city detection, and connection type. We use this data exclusively for:

  • Powering our city rankings and ISP comparisons
  • Contributing to Pakistan's digital divide research
  • Helping users make informed ISP decisions

Why PakSpeed is Different

We strictly prioritize user experience and measurement accuracy. PakSpeed operates securely over HTTPS through the Cloudflare Edge network, meaning test servers sit at major internet exchange points ensuring your results reflect the actual backbone performance of your ISP (PTCL, Nayatel, StormFiber, Jazz, Zong, Telenor, etc.) rather than an arbitrary remote server overseas.

Key Differentiators

  • Local Edge Testing: Unlike global speed tests connecting to overseas servers, PakSpeed tests against Cloudflare PoPs within Pakistan.
  • Bilingual (Urdu + English): Pakistan's first internet speed test with full Urdu localization and Nastaliq typography.
  • ISP Comparison: Real-time ISP rankings and city-level breakdowns so you can compare providers in your area.
  • Open Data: Our city leaderboard and speed guide help users understand what their results mean.
  • No App Required: Fully browser-based PWA. Works on any device with a modern browser.
