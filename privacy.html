Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 3, 2026

Welcome to PakSpeed (the "Service"), operated by WANG and Urdu AI. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it when you use our internet speed testing tools.

1. Information We Collect

Speed Test Data: When you initiate a speed test, we collect network performance metrics (download speed, upload speed, ping, jitter). We also collect your IP address to determine your Internet Service Provider (ISP).

Location Data: If you explicitly click "Update Exact Location" and grant browser permissions, we collect your precise GPS coordinates (Latitude and Longitude) to populate our public Community Speed Map. If you decline, we only use approximate IP-based location (City level).

2. How We Use Information

We use the data collected to:

  • Provide and improve the core speed testing accuracy.
  • Aggregate anonymous geographic data to display on our Community Speed Map.
  • Generate statistical reports on internet infrastructure health across Pakistan.

3. Cookies and Tracking (AdSense)

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies (like the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your personal identifying information. Telemetry data displayed publicly on our dashboard and maps is always stripped of exact IP addresses and presented as aggregated demographic network metrics.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@pakspeed.com. PakSpeed is operated by WALI (WANG Lab of Innovation) and the Urdu AI development team.
